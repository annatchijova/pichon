# PICHÓN — Soviet absurd cinema + old-Runet digital madness

Companion to `deck/ideas-locas-presentacion.md`. Two new source veins for the deck and the site:

- **Vein A — philosophical-absurd cinema** (Kin-dza-dza!, Dva Kapitana 2, Loban, Kvartet I, DMB, the 90s gangster comedies): bureaucratic absurdism, deadpan mockumentary, systems that run on rules nobody can explain.
- **Vein B — 2000s Runet "digital madness"** (udaff.com era, }{0ТТ@БЬ)Ч, leetspeak, IRC logs, web-1.0 artifacts): the aesthetic of special services hunting technology they cannot parse — which is PICHÓN's whole premise.

**Decode-budget rule** (applies to everything below): the audience in Buenos Aires will not have seen any of these films. The reference works at the *structural* level — a Kin-dza-dza pants-color caste system is funny without knowing Kin-dza-dza. Quote-literal material lives only in **Dahgoth's untranslated Russian channel** (see §3 of the main doc): he is the only character who gets the references, which is itself the joke.

## A. Kin-dza-dza! — the doctrine kit

The richest vein. Plyuk is PICHÓN: a fully self-consistent civilization whose rules are arbitrary and enforced with total seriousness.

1. **Pants-color classification → species clearance levels.** On Plyuk, status is the color of your pants. PICHÓN adopts this literally: the classification labels from the main doc's §1 (`NO CLASIFICADO / RESTRINGIDO / CONFIDENCIAL / CÓSMICO`) get a color code, and one slide states the rule with a straight face: `EL NIVEL DE ACCESO SE DETERMINA POR EL COLOR DEL PANTALÓN DEL AGENTE` (Access level is determined by the color of the agent's pants). Dahgoth wears the highest color. Nobody explains the mapping.
2. **`Ку!` greeting protocol.** The universal Plyuk greeting. On the Russian slide (main doc §3), the tiny Spanish word is no longer `SÍ` — it's `КУ!` left untranslated. Alternatively: the title slide gets a protocol line: `SALUDO OFICIAL: КУ` (Official greeting). Audience will say "cu" — that is already a win.
3. **Gravitsappa = the MacGuffin of the method.** On S26 (how PICHÓN decides), add the device line: the MaxSAT engine runs on a `ГРАВИЦАППА` — one word, in Cyrillic, unexplained, in the middle of an otherwise technical slide. It's the Kin-dza-dza device that makes travel between galaxies possible; here it's the component that makes the doctrine possible. `SIN GRAVITSAPPA NO HAY VEREDICTO`.
4. **Chatlanians vs Patsakians → palomas vs cuervos.** Two peoples, externally identical, at eternal war, distinguished by a light that flashes orange or green. This is *already* the paloma/cuervo dynamic (S8, S28 "los cuervos discreparon"). One added line on the org chart makes it explicit: `EXTERIORMENTE IDÉNTICAS. LA DIFERENCIA LA MARCA EL DISPOSITIVO.` (Externally identical. The device marks the difference.)
5. **Эцилоп naming.** Plyuk's police is "police" spelled backwards. PICHÓN gets a reversed-word unit: `UNIDAD NÓHCIP` (PICHÓN backwards) — the internal-affairs division that watches the watchers. Fits the cuervos.

## B. Dva Kapitana 2 — the mockumentary register

Debizhev's film is a solemn pseudo-documentary about events that never happened, narrated with total historical gravity, starring two captains who "selflessly resist the forces of chaos, maintaining the cosmic balance of history."

1. **Anna and Dahgoth are the two captains.** The film's official synopsis is a ready-made PICHÓN mission statement. On S4 (the team), add the film's synopsis structure, translated: `DOS CAPITANES SE OPONEN DESINTERESADAMENTE A LAS FUERZAS DEL CAOS Y MANTIENEN EL EQUILIBRIO CÓSMICO DE LA HISTORIA` (Two captains selflessly oppose the forces of chaos and maintain the cosmic balance of history). It lands even if nobody gets the reference.
2. **Archival-chronicle treatment for Incidente 8821 (S28).** Restyle the incident as a fake Soviet documentary chronicle: grainy frame, dates in the corner, solemn captions, "found footage" of a pigeon at an antenna. The incident report becomes a historical document about an event that happened *this morning*.
3. **The collage technique.** Dva Kapitana 2 mixes real archival footage with staged scenes and lets the mismatch do the joking. Same trick for the deck: real CIA pigeon-program documents (the program is real — it's already on S6) interleaved with PICHÓN's fake ones, formatted identically. The audience cannot tell where the archive ends and the doctrine begins. That *is* the thesis of the deck.

## C. Loban double feature — Pyl + Shapito-show

1. **The Pyl mirror = the game's final stamp.** In *Dust*, special services recruit a nobody for an experiment; under mysterious radiation he looks in a mirror and sees a magnificent version of himself — and spends the rest of the film trying to see it again. The game's final stamp `USTED ES LA PALOMA` is exactly this mirror: the evaluated sees his true form for one second and can never unsee it. Proposal: the result screen of `juego.html` (or its slide version) gets a one-line flavor text in this spirit: `USTED VIO SU VERDADERA FORMA DURANTE 0,4 SEGUNDOS` (You saw your true form for 0.4 seconds).
2. **"We are all dust."** The film's thesis — everyone is dust, dust is the general error of perception — converts directly into doctrine: `LA PALOMA TAMPOCO ES UNA PALOMA. TODO ES POLVO.` (The pigeon isn't a pigeon either. Everything is dust.) A late-deck line, possibly on the black final page, before "removed by the pigeons". Philosophical absurdism at full volume, zero explanation.
3. **Киберстранник (Cyber-Wanderer) = Dahgoth's alias.** Shapito-show's Киберстранник is a man who exists almost entirely online and is treated as a legend by people who've never met him. Dahgoth — the double agent who only speaks Russian and whose translation is "unavailable for operational reasons" — gets this as his codename in the deck's Russian channel. His profile on S4 gains one Cyrillic line: `КИБЕРСТРАННИК`.
4. **Burn the circus.** Every Shapito-show story ends at the shapito, and the shapito burns. Structural license for the approved black ending (main doc §10): the deck's last page isn't a conclusion, it's the fire. `EL CHAPITEAU ARDIÓ. ESTO ES NORMAL.` (The circus tent burned down. This is normal.)

## D. Kvartet I pair — Election Day + Radio Day

1. **The doctrine is elected.** Election Day's core joke: an election manufactured out of nothing by operatives who don't believe in anything. S26 already contains `Empates: prevalece la creencia` (Ties: belief prevails) — make the electoral logic explicit with one line: `LA DOCTRINA NO SE DERIVA. SE ELIGE. SIEMPRE GANA EL MISMO CANDIDATO.` (The doctrine isn't derived. It's elected. The same candidate always wins.)
2. **Radio PICHÓN.** Radio Day is a live broadcast assembled in real time out of chaos — perfect skin for the guion's live segments. Proposal: a "breaking broadcast" slide style — red `EN VIVO` bug, ticker at the bottom, "SIGNAL ACQUIRED · PALOMA-8821 ON THE PERIMETER". The live game section (S14–S24) can be framed as a live emergency broadcast: each case is a newsflash, the audience's hand-vote is "calls from listeners", the verdict is read on air. The guion already plays this scene; the slides just dress for it.
3. **The assembled reality.** Both films are about people manufacturing an official reality under deadline pressure. That's the deck's tone rule from the guion ("la máquina hace el chiste; ustedes informan"). Add to speaker notes: when in doubt, present like a news anchor who personally finds none of this funny.

## E. DMB — the regulations manual

The army-comedy trick: regulations quoted verbatim, applied to situations they were never written for, enforced with total conviction.

1. **PICHÓN regulations slide** (`REGLAMENTO INTERNO`): 4–5 rules in regulation-manual style, each formally correct and operationally useless. Draft: `§1. ESTÁ PROHIBIDO MIRAR A LAS PALOMAS SIN AUTORIZACIÓN. §2. EL PAN ES MATERIAL CLASIFICADO. §3. EN CASO DE CONTACTO, NO CORRA: LA PALOMA ES MÁS RÁPIDA. §4. LA DUDA ES UNA FORMA DE CONTACTO.` (Forbidden to look at pigeons without authorization / Bread is classified material / In case of contact, do not run: the pigeon is faster / Doubt is a form of contact.)
2. Placement: pairs naturally with the approved "Training manual, page 47" slide (main doc §8.5) — one is the public manual, one is the internal reglamento.

## G. Khochu v tyurmu — voluntary files

A man who works hard to get himself imprisoned is the perfect PICHÓN citizen. The approved print form (main doc §6, "DENUNCIE SU PALOMA") gets a companion form: **`SOLICITE SU PROPIO EXPEDIENTE`** (Request your own file). Fields: `¿Por qué sospecha de usted mismo?` (Why do you suspect yourself?), `¿Desde cuándo?` (Since when?), `¿Con qué frecuencia come pan?` (How often do you eat bread?). The game's ending already tells the player a file has been opened on them — this form lets them *apply* for it. Peak Khochu-v-tyurmu.

## H. Osobennosti natsionalnoy… — the title-format parody

The *Peculiarities of the National Hunt/Fishing* franchise: the hunt is always a pretext for something else, nothing is ever caught, and the "peculiarities" are the national soul. Title-format slide (chapter divider): **`PECULIARIDADES DE LA CONTRAINTELIGENCIA NACIONAL`** — used as the section header before the game block (S14). The framing fits perfectly: in those films the ritual is the point, not the prey; in PICHÓN the evaluation is the point, not the pigeons. One deadpan subtitle: `NO SE HA CAPTURADO NADA DESDE 1986` (Nothing has been captured since 1986) — the Kin-dza-dza year, quietly.

## J. Daun Haus — the canon re-read by the org

Dostoevsky's *Idiot* re-staged in 90s chaos is the same operation PICHÓN performs: a serious canonical text processed by an insane but rigorous system. One slide: **`BIBLIOGRAFÍA OFICIAL DE LA DIRECCIÓN`** — three classics with official verdicts: `EL IDIOTA — F. DOSTOIEVSKI — VEREDICTO: EL IDIOTA ERA USTED` / `CRIMEN Y CASTIGO — VEREDICTO: SIN CARGOS, EL AVE ACTUÓ SOLA` / `1984 — VEREDICTO: OPTIMISTA`. (The idiot was you / No charges, the bird acted alone / Optimistic.) Small print: `TODA LA LITERATURA UNIVERSAL SERÁ REEVALUADA`.

## K. }{0ТТ@БЬ)Ч — Vein B centerpiece

The 2006 Khottabych is the exact illustration the brief describes: special services hunt a hacker across the net while the actual anomaly — a 3,732-year-old genie — arrives in a clay jar **ordered from an online store**. The org chases the technology; the magic ships by courier.

1. **Leetspeak title variant.** The film's title is `}{0ТТ@БЬ)Ч`. PICHÓN gets one leetspeak rendering, exactly one place, unexplained: `}{1С}{0И` or `Р1СН0И́` on page 13 (the approved "nonexistent page") or as graffiti on the Russian slide. People who decode it get a point nobody awards.
2. **RTFM.** The film's best software joke: the genie reads the manual. The method slide (S12) gets the footer: `RTFM — RESOLUCIÓN INTERNA 001/26`. It already has "Resolución interna 001/26" — this just arms it.
3. **The jar arrives by courier.** The QR slide (S30) copy addition: the test link is delivered `POR MENSAJERO, EN UN JARRÓN DE ARCILLA` (by courier, in a clay jar). Nobody knows why the expediente travels like this. Operational reasons.
4. **The foreign specialist.** The FSB flies in "the best cybercrime agent" from America to catch Gena. This maps to Anna's role vs Dahgoth: the org imports expertise it doesn't understand to fight a threat it doesn't understand. Possible guion beat, not necessarily a slide.

## L. Mify — the org chart as mythology

A stranger lands in Moscow and, with zero evidence, maps everyone onto Greek gods. That is precisely the S8 operation: palomas = surveillance, cuervos = counterintelligence, avispas = firewall, Cordyceps = ransomware. Proposal: restyle (or duplicate) the org chart as a **pantheon** — each species gets a god-card with title and portfolio: `PALOMA, DIOSA DE LA VIGILANCIA URBANA · CULTOS: PLAZAS, BANCOS` / `CUERVO, DIOS DE LA AUDITORÍA · RESCATE: NINGUNO`. The approved "High-value targets" slide was dropped; this is its better replacement: mythology formatting *is* the org chart's true form.

## M. Vnuk Gagarina — reality redacts fiction

The film's meta-story is better than the film: it was released as "Gagarin's Grandson", Gagarin's daughters sued, and the court ordered the title changed and every mention redubbed. Fiction was literally censored by reality.

1. **A slide censored by court order.** One slide carries its original title struck through and a redubbed replacement, exactly like the film: `CASO: VNUK DEL COSMONAUTA` → ~~ГАГАРИНА~~ → `SUJETO NO IDENTIFICADO`, with a footnote: `TÍTULO MODIFICADO POR ORDEN JUDICIAL. LAS PALOMAS NO COMENTAN.` (Title modified by court order. The pigeons do not comment.)
2. **The unverifiable claim.** The plot engine — a kid claims to be Gagarin's grandson and the system must process the claim — is a ready-made extra game case: `SUJETO AFIRMA SER NIETO DE GAGARÍN. EVIDENCIA: NINGUNA. VEREDICTO: ESPÍA. NADIE HEREDA TANTA ÓRBITA POR CASUALIDAD.` (Subject claims to be Gagarin's grandson. Evidence: none. Verdict: spy. Nobody inherits that much orbit by accident.) Candidate for `juego.html` case 09, or a slide-only case in the FAQ block (main doc §8).
